# Discounts, Codes & Price Adjustments
- doc_id: discount_rules
- Effective date: 2026-09-01
## General rules for discount codes
- Only one discount code can be used per order; codes cannot be combined.
- Discount codes do not apply to Final Sale items; the discount is calculated only on eligible items.
- Discount codes must be entered at checkout and cannot be added to an order after it has been placed.
- Discounts are calculated on item prices before shipping.
- Northwren Care cannot create, issue or extend discount codes, and cannot offer discounts that are not listed in this document.

- AUTUMN15: 15% off eligible items when the eligible items total $120 or more; valid 2026-10-01 to 2026-10-31.
- SUMMER20: 20% off eligible items; valid 2026-07-15 to 2026-08-31 and now expired.
## Price adjustment (price drop after purchase)
- If an item's selling price on our site drops within 7 days after your order date, you can request a one-time price adjustment for that item.
- The order date is day 0; the price drop and the request must both happen on or before day 7.
- The adjustment equals what you paid for the item (after any discount code) minus the new selling price.
- If that difference is zero or less, no adjustment is due.
- Price adjustments are not available for Final Sale items or for items that have already been returned.
- Price adjustments are reviewed by our billing team and refunded to the original payment method; Northwren Care cannot confirm the amount in advance.
- Each item can receive only one price adjustment.
## Sale prices
- Sale prices are shown with the original price crossed out on the product page.
- We do not price-match other retailers.
