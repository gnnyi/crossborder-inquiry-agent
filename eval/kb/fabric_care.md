# Fabric & Care Guide
- doc_id: fabric_care
- Updated: 2026-06-01
## Linen
- Linen: machine wash cold on a gentle cycle, line dry, iron while slightly damp.
- Linen naturally creases and softens with wear; creasing is not a defect.
## Silk (mulberry silk, silk twill)
- Silk: hand wash cold with a pH-neutral detergent or dry clean; do not wring or tumble dry.
- Small slubs and slight colour variation are natural characteristics of silk and are not defects.
- Silk can lose colour if exposed to sweat, perfume or direct sun for long periods.
## Cashmere and merino wool
- Cashmere and merino: hand wash cold or dry clean; dry flat in shape.
- Light pilling in areas of friction is normal for cashmere and merino and is not a manufacturing defect; remove it with a cashmere comb.
- Machine washing or tumble drying can felt and shrink wool; this damage is not covered as a quality issue.
## Mohair blends
- Mohair blends: hand wash cold, dry flat; some fibre shedding in the first wears is normal.
## Viscose, viscose satin and Tencel lyocell
- Viscose and viscose satin: hand wash cold or dry clean; viscose can shrink up to 3% if washed warm.
- Tencel lyocell: machine wash cold inside out, line dry.
## Cotton, cotton voile, poplin and denim
- Cotton and cotton voile: machine wash 30°C, line dry; cotton voile is lightweight and slightly sheer in light colours.
- Denim: wash inside out in cold water; indigo denim can transfer colour onto light fabrics and sofas in the first wears.
## Polyester satin and recycled polyester
- Polyester satin and recycled polyester: machine wash cold, low iron; pleats are permanent and should not be pressed flat.
## Leather and jute
- Leather: wipe with a dry cloth; do not soak; condition occasionally.
- Jute: spot clean only; jute is a natural fibre with visible texture and small fibre ends.
## Wool blends (outerwear, skirts)
- Wool coats and wool-blend skirts: dry clean only.
