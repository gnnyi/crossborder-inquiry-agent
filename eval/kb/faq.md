# Frequently Asked Questions
- doc_id: faq
- Updated: 2026-09-15
## Payment and currency
- We accept Visa, Mastercard, American Express, PayPal and Apple Pay.
- All orders are charged in US dollars; your bank may convert the amount and charge a conversion fee, which we cannot refund.
- Payment is taken when the order is placed.
## Orders and account
- An order confirmation email is sent within 1 hour of payment; check your spam folder if it has not arrived.
- You can check out as a guest; an account is not required.
- The email address on an order cannot be changed after the order is placed.
## Stock
- Sold-out sizes can be followed with the "Notify me" button on the product page.
- We cannot reserve or hold items, and we cannot confirm restock dates.
## Gift cards
- Digital gift cards are available from $25 to $200 and are valid for 3 years.
- Gift cards cannot be exchanged for cash.
## About Northwren
- Northwren designs its collections in Shenzhen; garments are made by partner factories in Guangdong, China.
- We do not have physical stores.
- Northwren Care answers emails Monday to Friday, 9:00-18:00 China Standard Time (UTC+8), usually within 1 business day.
## Other
- We do not offer gift wrapping.
- We do not offer pre-orders.
