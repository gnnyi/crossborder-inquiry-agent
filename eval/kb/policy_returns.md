# Returns & Exchanges Policy
- doc_id: policy_returns
- Effective date: 2026-03-01
## Return window
- You can start a return within 30 days of the delivery date shown in the carrier's tracking.
- The delivery date is day 0; the last day to start a return is the 30th calendar day after delivery.
- Example: an order delivered on 1 October can be returned if the return is started on or before 31 October.
- A return counts as started when the request is submitted in the Returns Portal or sent by email to Northwren Care.
- After a return is started, the parcel must be handed to a carrier within 10 days.
- Requests started after the 30-day window cannot be accepted through the standard return process.
## Condition of returned items
- Items must be unworn, unwashed and returned with the original tags attached.
- Items with makeup, deodorant or perfume marks, or pet hair, cannot be accepted and will be sent back to the customer.
## Final sale
- Items marked Final Sale on the product page and in the order confirmation cannot be returned or exchanged.
- Final Sale items are still covered by the quality issue process if they arrive damaged or defective (see quality_sop).
## Refunds
- Refunds are issued to the original payment method only.
- Refunds are issued after the returned items arrive at our returns centre and pass inspection.
- Inspection is usually completed within 3 business days after the parcel arrives.
- After a refund is issued, banks and payment providers may take a further 5-10 business days to show it.
- Original shipping fees are not refunded, unless the return is due to a quality issue or our error.
- Northwren Care cannot confirm whether a refund will be approved, or its amount, before inspection is complete.
## Return shipping
- US customers can use a prepaid return label from the Returns Portal; $7.95 is deducted from the refund.
- Customers in the UK, EU, Canada and Australia arrange and pay for return shipping to the returns centre shown in the Returns Portal.
- We recommend a tracked service; we cannot refund returns that are lost in transit without tracking.
- US returns go to our returns partner in Ontario, California; all other returns go to our returns centre in Dongguan, China.
## Exchanges
- One free size exchange per item is available within the 30-day return window, subject to stock.
- Colour exchanges are not offered; return the item and place a new order instead.
- If the requested size is out of stock, the exchange is converted into a return for refund.
