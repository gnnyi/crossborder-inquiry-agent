# Shipping & Delivery Policy
- doc_id: policy_shipping
- Effective date: 2026-09-01
- This policy applies to orders placed on or after 2026-09-01.
## Where we ship
- We ship to the United States, the United Kingdom, EU countries, Canada and Australia.
- All orders ship from our warehouses in Shenzhen and Dongguan, China.
## Processing time
- Orders are packed and handed to the carrier within 1-3 business days after payment.
- Business days are Monday to Friday, excluding public holidays in mainland China.
## Shipping fees
- Standard shipping is free on orders of $99 or more, for every destination we ship to.
- The free shipping threshold is calculated on the order value after discounts and before shipping.
- Standard shipping below the threshold costs $8.95 to the US and $12.95 to the UK, EU, Canada and Australia.
- Express shipping costs $19.95 to the US and $29.95 to the UK, EU, Canada and Australia, and is never free.
## Estimated transit times (after dispatch)
- Standard to the US: 7-12 business days.
- Standard to the UK and EU: 8-14 business days.
- Standard to Canada and Australia: 10-16 business days.
- Express to all destinations: 4-7 business days.
- Transit times are estimates, not guarantees; we cannot promise a specific delivery date.
- Customs checks can add 2-7 business days to any shipment.
## Duties and taxes
- US orders: no import duties or taxes are charged to the customer.
- UK and EU orders are shipped Delivered Duty Paid: import VAT and duties are included in the price at checkout, so nothing is due on delivery.
- Canada and Australia orders: import duties and taxes, if any, are charged by the carrier on delivery and are paid by the recipient.
- Parcels refused because duties were not paid are returned to us; the refund excludes the original shipping fee and return freight.
## Tracking
- A tracking link is emailed when the order ships.
- Tracking may take up to 3 days after dispatch to show the first carrier scan.
- If tracking has not updated for 10 consecutive days, Northwren Care opens an investigation with the carrier.
- Carrier investigations take up to 15 business days; the outcome (reship or refund) is decided after the investigation.
## Delivered but not received
- If tracking shows delivered but the parcel is missing, please wait 48 hours and check with neighbours, building reception and any safe place.
- Missing parcels must be reported within 14 days of the delivery scan.
- We open a claim with the carrier; reshipping or refunding is decided after the carrier's response and cannot be promised in advance.
## Address problems
- Addresses can only be changed before an order ships (see order_changes).
- If the carrier reports an incomplete or wrong address, the parcel is usually held at the local depot for 5-7 days before being returned to sender.
- Address corrections after dispatch are requested from the carrier and are not guaranteed.
