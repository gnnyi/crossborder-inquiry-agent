# Shipping & Delivery Policy
- doc_id: policy_shipping_2025
- Effective date: 2025-03-01
## Where we ship
- We ship to the United States, the United Kingdom, EU countries, Canada and Australia.
- All orders ship from our warehouses in Shenzhen and Dongguan, China.
## Processing time
- Orders are packed and handed to the carrier within 1-3 business days after payment.
## Shipping fees
- Standard shipping to the US is free on orders of $79 or more; below $79 it costs $8.95.
- Standard shipping to the UK, EU, Canada and Australia is free on orders of $129 or more; below $129 it costs $12.95.
- Express shipping costs $19.95 to the US and $29.95 to all other destinations, and is never free.
## Estimated transit times (after dispatch)
- Standard to the US: 10-18 business days.
- Standard to the UK, EU, Canada and Australia: 12-20 business days.
- Express to all destinations: 5-8 business days.
- Transit times are estimates, not guarantees.
## Duties and taxes
- Prices do not include import duties or taxes for destinations outside the US.
- For UK, EU, Canada and Australia orders, any import VAT, duties or customs fees are charged on delivery and paid by the recipient.
## Tracking
- A tracking link is emailed when the order ships.
- If tracking has not updated for 10 consecutive days, Northwren Care opens an investigation with the carrier.
