# Quality Issue Handling (SOP)
- doc_id: quality_sop
- Updated: 2026-08-01
## Reporting window and evidence
- Quality issues must be reported within 14 days of the delivery date (delivery date is day 0).
- A report needs the order number, a photo of the issue, a photo of the care label, and a photo of the whole item laid flat.
- If photos are missing, the case stays open for 7 days while we wait for them.
- Reports received after 14 days are reviewed case by case by a team lead; approval is not guaranteed.
## What counts as a quality issue
- Covered: broken seams, holes, stains or marks present on arrival, broken zips or buttons, wrong item sent, wrong size sent compared with the order, missing item.
- Not covered: light pilling, natural creasing of linen, natural slubs in silk or linen, damage from washing against the care label, normal wear.
- Final Sale items are covered by this process like any other item.
## Review and outcome
- The QC team reviews each report within 2 business days of receiving complete photos.
- The outcome can be a replacement, a full or partial refund, or a return for inspection; the QC team decides the outcome.
- Northwren Care must not promise a specific outcome (replacement, refund amount or compensation) before the QC decision.
- When a quality return is approved, Northwren pays the return shipping.
## Wrong or missing items
- For a reported missing or wrong item, the warehouse checks the packing record and parcel weight within 2 business days.
