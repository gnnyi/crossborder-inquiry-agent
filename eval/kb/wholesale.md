# Wholesale & Bulk Orders
- doc_id: wholesale
- Updated: 2026-05-15
- Northwren sells wholesale to registered retailers and boutiques.
- All wholesale, bulk (more than 10 units of one style), distribution, dropshipping and custom-label enquiries are handled by the Wholesale team.
- The Wholesale team's address is wholesale@northwren.example.
- Northwren Care does not quote wholesale prices, bulk discounts, minimum order quantities or production lead times.
- To apply, send the Wholesale team your business name, website or shop address, country, tax or VAT number, and the styles you are interested in.
- The Wholesale team replies within 3 business days.
- Personal orders of up to 10 units of one style can be placed on the website at retail price.
